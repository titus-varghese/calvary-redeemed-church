About the church

Who we are

We are a small group of passionate Christians who seek to worship the Triune God, to live in obedience to His Word, to grow in fellowship with God and with our fellow brethren, and to be witnesses for our Lord Jesus Christ where He has placed us.

Most of us make our dwelling in the satellite city of Navi Mumbai, and we gather for worship at our church there.

While we function independently as a local church, led by a plurality of elders, we are connected to the historic church called out as the Body of our Lord even before the foundations of the world.

While independent in our functioning as the local assembly, we are part of and accountable to the great Universal Church which is primarily the Body of Christ and made up of all the redeemed children of God in the current age, and the ages gone by and the ages to come.

We subscribe to the great Ecumenical creeds (Apostles, Nicene, Chalcedonian) and the great Confessions (Westminster, Belgic, Heidelberg) and trace our connection to church through the ages, going back to our Lord and His Apostles and beyond.

We are imperfect, weak, striving people, but we are by Grace justified and redeemed, placed on the path of Sanctification and final glorification. We are Trinitarians and mostly Reformed in Theology. We gather on the Lord's day (Sunday) to sing and praise Him which culminates in the administration of the Communion or Lord's table. Communion is not only a memorial and symbol for us, rather we believe in the real, Spiritual presence of our Lord in the Communion, as we partake of Him. (This is in accordance with the classical Protestant view of the Reformers.)

After having offered our praises to the Triune God, and partaking of His presence in the Lord's Body and blood, we listen to His instructions, admonitions and encouragement through the ministry of the Word.

While we practise the baptism of confessing individuals we share in the communion with all those who confess the faith “once for all delivered to the saints”.

We believe in the continued work of The Holy Spirit and the continuation of the gifts as seen in the Scriptures and operated according to the Sovereign will and purpose of the Holy Spirit because He is God. All gifts should be humbly used for the edifying of the Church and not for the projection of individual authority and power or any other base considerations.

Please read our statement of faith to understand the core doctrines which we hold dearly.

Statement of faith

What we believe

  1. 01

    The Triune God

    We believe in the Triune God, eternally in three persons, The Father, The Son and The Holy Spirit.

  2. 02

    The Virgin Birth

    We believe in the virgin birth of our Lord Jesus Christ. We believe and affirm that the Second Person of the Triune Godhead, The Son who was incarnate in human flesh, was named Jesus the Christ, having been conceived by the Holy Spirit in the womb of Virgin Mary.

  3. 03

    Death and Resurrection

    We believe in the crucifixion, death and burial of the Lord Jesus Christ and His subsequent resurrection three days later.

  4. 04

    The Church

    We believe in the one holy, universal, church built on the foundation of the Apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the chief cornerstone.

  5. 05

    The Local Church

    We affirm that every believer should be part of a local church for his/her spiritual growth and to fellowship with other saints.

  6. 06

    Holy Scripture

    We believe that the 39 books of the Old Testament and the 27 books of the New Testament as God-breathed Word of God and sufficient and necessary for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness.

  7. 07

    The Sacraments

    We affirm that two Sacraments were instituted by our Lord (namely Baptism and Lord's Supper) and given to the church in which all of God's people should be partakers.

  8. 08

    Salvation

    We believe that salvation for eternal life is a free gift of God, given to His elect, by His grace, and received by faith alone.

  9. 09

    The Return of Christ

    We believe and look forward to the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ in glory, to judge the living and the dead, the righteous in Christ and the wicked.

  10. 10

    Eternal Life

    We believe that those who are justified in Christ will resurrect to eternal life in the presence of God.
